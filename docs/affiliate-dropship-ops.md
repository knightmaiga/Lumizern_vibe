# Affiliate + Dropship Operating Model

## Product Type Rules

### A) Native/Dropship Product
- `is_affiliate = no`
- Standard Woo add-to-cart and checkout
- Keep stock and shipping profiles accurate

### B) Affiliate Product
- `_is_affiliate = yes`
- `_affiliate_url` required
- Button text: `View Product →`
- Add `target="_blank"` and `rel="nofollow sponsored"`
- Show affiliate badge in product loops

## Merchandising Rules
- Each vibe should have:
  - 12–30 products at launch
  - mix of price anchors (low, mid, high)
  - at least 2 hero products

## Weekly Operations
1. Add 10–20 products from supplier/affiliate feeds.
2. Assign vibes and add persuasive short descriptions.
3. Test links and conversion elements.
4. Remove low-CTR products.
5. Refresh top vibe pages with new arrivals.

## KPI Dashboard (minimum)
- Vibe quiz completion rate
- Product click-through by vibe
- Add-to-cart rate by vibe
- Checkout initiation rate
- Purchase conversion rate
- Affiliate outbound CTR
