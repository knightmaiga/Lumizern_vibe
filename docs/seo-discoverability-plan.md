# SEO & Discoverability Plan

## 1) Information Architecture
- Core money pages:
  - `/shop/`
  - `/vibe/{slug}/` for all vibes
  - top product categories
- Content cluster pages:
  - vibe guides
  - comparisons
  - gift/use-case pages

## 2) On-page Requirements
Every page must include:
- Intent-aligned title tag
- CTR-optimized meta description
- One H1 only
- Structured H2/H3 hierarchy
- Internal links to 2–5 related pages
- Product or article schema where applicable

## 3) Content Cadence (first 90 days)
- Publish 3–4 posts/week
- Mix:
  - 40% buying-intent posts
  - 40% vibe lifestyle guides
  - 20% comparisons/roundups

## 4) Internal Linking Rules
- Each blog post links to:
  - 1 vibe page
  - 2 product/category pages
  - 1 related blog post
- Each vibe page links to:
  - related guides
  - top 6 products of that vibe

## 5) Technical SEO Baseline
- Fast hosting + caching + image compression
- Preload fonts, minimize render-blocking CSS/JS
- Ensure canonical tags and noindex for thin pages
- Fix broken links weekly

## 6) Authority Growth (free-first)
- Build social proof and UGC on Pinterest/Instagram/TikTok
- Repurpose articles into social snippets with linkbacks
- Submit brand to relevant directories and niche communities
